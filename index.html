About

I'm a communications graduate and writer with a recent Bachelor of Communications (Creative Writing), and I've spent years making content about the things I actually care about. My podcast, Do You Still Like Us, is about being on the spectrum — it's lived-experience content, made by someone who is the audience, not an outsider looking in.

Alongside that I write about Japanese pro wrestling for Monthly Puroresu, run the Wrestlewolf YouTube channel, and have had fiction published in Talent Implied 2022. Before the writing, I spent thirty years in customer-facing work, which taught me more about audiences than any textbook — and more about why I'd rather be the one writing the script than reading it down a phone.

I'm looking for writing, editing, content, or production work — especially in accessibility, disability communications, or media — where the work is judged on what gets made rather than on how well I perform in a room.
